Kazakhstan

Қазақстан

Central Asia

The world’s ninth-largest country and its largest landlocked one, stretching from the Caspian Sea to the Altai mountains.

ConnectionHamza lived and worked here from 2022 to 2025.

Interesting facts

  • It was the last Soviet republic to declare independence, on 16 December 1991.
  • Yuri Gagarin’s Vostok 1 lifted off from the Baikonur Cosmodrome in Kazakhstan in April 1961. Russia leases the site until 2050.
  • Wild apples, Malus sieversii, grow in its mountains. They are the main wild ancestor of the cultivated apple.
  • Almaty, the largest city, was the capital until 1997.
