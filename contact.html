Collaboration and supervision

I welcome collaboration with researchers in academia and industry on the physical layer of 6G, especially:

  • optimization and performance analysis of RIS, STAR-RIS, fluid-antenna and movable-antenna systems
  • UAV-enabled and aerial-RIS networks
  • non-orthogonal and rate-splitting multiple access
  • integrated sensing and communication, and physical-layer security
  • design that accounts for transceiver hardware impairments
  • learning-based methods for resource allocation, such as deep reinforcement learning

I am glad to co-supervise PhD, master’s and undergraduate students, and to host visiting researchers and interns. If you are interested, email me your CV, a few lines on the topic you would like to work on, and, if you have one, a paper or report you have written.

Visiting researchers and interns usually need their own funding, for example a scholarship or an exchange programme.
