The four links

Vehicle-to-everything (V2X) connects a car to other cars, to the roadside, to people and to the network. Each link serves different jobs, from collision warnings to software updates.

  • V2VCollision warnings, platooning and cooperative manoeuvres.
  • V2IRoadside units at junctions, traffic lights and toll points.
  • V2PPhones and wearables of people near the road.
  • V2NTraffic information, maps, updates and entertainment.

Two families of standards

One family grew out of Wi-Fi, the other out of cellular networks.

More detail: sidelink modes

In NR-V2X, sidelink resources are either scheduled by the base station (mode 1) or chosen by the vehicles themselves (mode 2), which keeps the sidelink working outside network coverage. Release 16 added unicast and groupcast to the broadcast of LTE-V2X.

Mobility and Doppler

Speed is what makes V2X hard. A moving receiver sees each path shifted in frequency, by an amount that depends on the angle it arrives from. The paths beat against each other, and the signal fades in time.

Blockage, RIS and drones

Vehicles are themselves obstacles. A lorry between two cars can block the line of sight for seconds.

A RIS can also protect vehicular links from eavesdroppers by focusing energy on the legitimate vehicle and away from others. The papers below study dual-RIS vehicle-to-infrastructure links, RIS-aided security for vehicular NOMA and aerial RIS deployment. RIS tutorial

Key takeaways
  • V2X covers V2V, V2I, V2P and V2N links, over the sidelink (PC5) and the cellular interface (Uu).
  • Doppler shift grows with speed and carrier frequency; coherence time falls well below a millisecond at mmWave.
  • Out-of-date channel knowledge limits every technique that depends on it.
  • Roadside and aerial RIS can route around vehicle blockage.

Check your understanding

A car drives at 90 km/h. What is the maximum Doppler shift at 3.5 GHz?

v = 25 m/s, so fD = 25 × 3.5×10⁹ / 3×10⁸ ≈ 292 Hz.

Why does the coherence time matter for RIS-assisted V2X?

RIS phases are computed from channel estimates. If the channel changes before the surface is reconfigured, the reflected paths no longer add in phase and the gain drops.

Which 3GPP interface carries direct vehicle-to-vehicle links?

The sidelink, PC5.
